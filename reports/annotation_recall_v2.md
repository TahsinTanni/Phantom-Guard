# Labeler recall: annotation sheet (v2)

40 labeler-negative campaigns of `data/frozen/incidents_snapshot_v2.jsonl` for an estimate of what the malware labeler misses. Generated by `python -m src.evaluation.annotation_sheets`; sample record `results/annotation_sample_v2.json`. The six mechanisms:

- `obfuscated_payload_execution`
- `hidden_install_hook`
- `credential_or_wallet_exfiltration`
- `remote_backdoor_access`
- `remote_payload_retrieval`
- `brand_impersonation_narrative`

## Rule

The labeler found no mechanism in this text. Verdict **PRESENT** if the prose describes at least one of the six mechanisms listed above (the labeler missed it), **ABSENT** if it describes none or names a mechanism only in an analyst tag list and not in prose (same tag-list rule as the precision sheet), and **UNSURE** otherwise. For PRESENT, name the category or categories in the Note.

## What is in the sheet

- **Frame:** the 137 DV-negative campaigns (of 148) that are not no-text (11 no-text campaigns excluded: their only text is the OpenSSF credit link).
- **Sample:** stratified by ecosystem × Amazon Inspector coverage, proportional allocation, `random.Random(20261006)`. Drawn/frame per stratum: npm|amazon 15/51, npm|no-amazon 6/19, pypi|amazon 9/32, pypi|no-amazon 10/35. Rows are ordered by ecosystem, stratum, then package name.
- **Text:** the full summary and description of every record (GHSA first, then OSV) of the package shown, which is the campaign's first member. This is the text the labeler read.
- No grammar flag and no judgement is printed. Fill in **Verdict** and **Note** on each row. Annotate without looking at the other annotator's sheet.
